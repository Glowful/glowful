Glowful

This is a place to put those fun things you really want to do and keep coming back to.

If you keep saying "bake a souffle" then the souffle will start to glow more! You can also adjust item glow manually.

View stuff on a star map or as a list!

Data is stored locally, which you can export and import — it is recommended to export to "save" your stuff.
